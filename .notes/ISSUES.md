# Open issues

- Under the PBR profile the handheld's tour at `ee7852dc50` flies all 93 places but past its memory: the process's anonymous memory peaked at 5,581 MiB with 1,376 MiB of it swapped out, at leg 78, where glibc's heap held 2,008 MiB free of the 5,690 MiB it had taken; the images were loaded 46,822 times, 37,270 of them again, and the tour took 994.5 s against the desk's 89.6.
- The handheld's content pool refuses small resources while it has room free in pieces: on the Fullrest Repack tour of a build ahead of `3ab057cd39`, from leg 17 on, 859 takes of 0.4–8 MiB were refused (bottom-level structures of 0.8–1.6 MiB the most), each with 78–92 MiB free in the pool's blocks but no range larger than 0.8–4.7 MiB, and no room for a new block under content's ceiling, while 2,056–2,112 textures rested on 904–910 MiB and 7,031–7,731 meshes on 1,238–1,536 MiB; a mesh was left out at -13,-28 and a texture drew the stand-in at 7,-34. The room an arrival's eviction is sized against counts the blocks' free bytes whole, scattered or not (`MemoryAllocator::getRoom`, `SceneUploader::evictForRoom`).
- The handheld's Fullrest Repack tour at `8c032f6e00`, with the eviction for what waits, still refuses content: 325 textures of 0.4–5.4 MiB and 6 structure blocks of 64 MiB, the process 1.1–2.1 GiB under its budget, beside 2,269 resting textures on 930 MiB and 9,235 resting meshes on 2,121 MiB; a texture drew the stand-in and a mesh was left out. Its heap ended with 4,642 MiB free of 9,364 MiB taken, in arenas nine tenths free, and 3,202 MiB swapped out. Its first place flown again at the end held 4,683,631 vertices in use against 3,060,436 and 719.6 MiB of live structures against 499.9, for the same instances and textures; `textures-readable` failed at 147 stops from leg 9 on; its worst frame was 3,939.5 ms.
- A texture's arrival reads and decodes its source file before it asks the content store, and again at every re-arrival: the store's key is a digest of the source's texels (`ChainPass::digest`), which the host lets go of once the device has its copy (`PreparedImages::serve`, `SceneUploader::hand`).
- A texture's want never matches a resting texture of its own size: the hole adds alignment and granularity margins (`holeFor`) that the fit, the image's own range, does not carry (`RestQueue::firstFitting`).
- A static mesh refused in the loose structure storage files no want (`BottomLevelStore`, `if (kept)`), so nothing is evicted for it.
- `Resource::CacheBudget::derive` counts as taken what the heap holds free, and `evictToBudget` evicts on the inserting thread, the frame's included, inside the budget's lock.
- `MWWorld::Scene::preloadTeleportDoorDestinations` loads a destination's references (`findCell`) before `CellPreloader::preload` asks the budget, so a preload skipped has still loaded the cell.
- `cppcoreguidelines-avoid-non-const-global-variables` finds 175 variables of mutable global state over the tree (OpenAL's extension pointers in `openaloutput.cpp`, `stats.cpp`'s `collectStatEvent`, the editor's `defaultgmsts.hpp` tables, test fixtures among them), each without an owner.

- `google-default-arguments` finds 363 default arguments on virtual functions and their overrides (`MWWorld::Class` and its subclasses, `MWBase::StateManager`, the editor's models), where a call takes the default of the static type it names.
- `components/platform/filestdio.cpp`, a third implementation of `Platform::File`, is built by no target.
