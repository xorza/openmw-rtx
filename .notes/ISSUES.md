# Open issues

- On the Fullrest Repack, `bench --suite=streaming` draws a frame of 31–37 ms at a cell crossing, in the `update` row, against a median frame of 5.0 ms and a p99 of 18.7, on a route flown at 12,000 units a second. The frame is upstream's synchronous cell change, which took 6.5–29 ms of the main thread at a crossing, and about 10 ms at most crossings. Of it, inserting the cells is 60% (the NPCs' rendering 21%, a levelled list's spawned creature 17.5%, statics 9%, creatures 8.7% and physics 8%), with cloning each object's scene graph 18%: the preloader readies templates and collision shapes and no scene graph instance. The unload is 14.5%, the window manager's cell change 8.4% (the local map's markers 4.5%), the navigator 7.5% and the fork's code 3%. Models the preloader had not reached load on the main thread: 86 over the run, 17 ms in all, sheathed weapons, animated creatures and flora among them.
