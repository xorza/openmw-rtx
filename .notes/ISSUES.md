# Open issues

- `RtxVisibilityKernelsTest.noKernelSpillsPastItsCeiling` fails on a low-end AMD integrated GPU under AMD's Windows driver: it reads the `RTX_DARK_ALPHA` twin of each stage, where `water` holds 244 registers against 219, `bounce` 144 against 128, and `panes` 240 bytes of scratch against 192. The twin the game nearly always draws held 221, 128 and 80 at `0879f5f62d`, and no test reads it.
- `rtx.gpu.0` and `rtx.gpu.1` pass the 300 seconds of the test preset's timeout on a low-end AMD integrated GPU under AMD's Windows driver when the pipelines compile for the first time. The same binary, compiled already, takes 74 seconds.
- `crash.matrix` failed once on a low-end AMD integrated GPU under AMD's Windows driver, in `hang`, `recovers-before-end` and `ends-before-end`, beside GPU suites compiling their pipelines: the program paused about 15 seconds and reported a second hang, or wrote no package. Three runs of it alone passed.
- `RtxStructureStorageTest.aBlockTheDeviceHasNoRoomForIsAskedForAgainAtTheStructuresSize` failed once in five full `rtx-gpu-tests` runs on a low-end AMD integrated GPU under AMD's Windows driver, with `a device with no budget extension cannot say what the heap holds`: `Testing::budgetAbove` read a `mHeld` of nought on a device that passed the same test in the other four runs.
- `motionOf` stores a non-finite `farther` where a ray commits a hit at a distance of nought and nothing moved: `0 / 0`. On a low-end AMD integrated GPU under AMD's Windows driver, the triangle `Testing::addModel` lays through the eye of `RtxFramesTest`'s `ahead()` camera gives every such ray that hit, in `RtxFramesTest.aPicturesSceneLetGoGivesBackEveryAllocation`; a high-end NVIDIA laptop GPU gives none.
- A picture's non-finite stores stay in the census until the next frame reads it, so they are charged to that frame: under `GTEST_SHUFFLE`, to whichever test draws next (`RtxPenumbraTest.aFillCastsAPenumbraAsWideAsItsWholeBall`, `RtxPenumbraDenoiseTest.theShadowDenoiserTakesTheNoiseOffAPenumbraAndLeavesItsLightWhereItWas`, `RtxVisibilityTest.aTranslucentOccluderDimsTheSunRatherThanStoppingIt`).
