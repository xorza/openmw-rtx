What it is

OpenMW RTX is a fork of OpenMW with a ray tracer in place of its renderer. The whole picture is ray traced on the GPU: visibility, shadows, direct and indirect light, sky, water and fog.

OpenMW stays the engine underneath, with its cells, physics, scripts, animation, weather and interface. The goal is a 2002 game made to look astonishing on current hardware, with its content as it stands.

Vanilla Morrowind and the PBR replacers made for OpenMW are read as they are. Morrowind's textures have their light painted in, so the renderer estimates that light and divides it out to recover materials the new light can use.

Written and tested on NVIDIA first; AMD cards are less tested. FSR upscaling is built in, and on an NVIDIA RTX card the launcher can fetch DLSS Ray Reconstruction from NVIDIA instead.
